# Agile Methodologies 🚀

This section outlines key Agile practices and techniques for improving workflows and team efficiency.

---

## **1. User Stories 📜**

User Stories capture requirements from the user’s perspective, ensuring features meet user needs.

**Format**:  
- **As a** [persona],  
- **I want** [feature]  
- **So that** [value].

**Techniques**:
- **32 Technique (Who, What, Why)**: Identify the user, feature, and purpose. 
- **3C Technique (Card, Conversation, Confirmation)**: Concise stories with clear acceptance criteria.
- **INVEST**: Ensure stories are Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Sized Appropriately, and Testable.

---

## **2. Backlog Management 📋**

Backlog Management ensures the team focuses on the most valuable tasks by continuously prioritizing and refining the backlog.

**Key Practices**:
- **Prioritize**: Rank tasks by value and urgency.
- **Refine**: Break down large tasks into smaller, actionable ones.

---

## **3. Refinement & Prioritization of Tasks 🔄**

Refinement prepares tasks for development, while prioritization ensures that the most valuable tasks are worked on first.

**Techniques**:
- **MoSCoW**: Prioritize by Must Have, Should Have, Could Have, and Won’t Have.
- **Value vs. Effort Matrix**: Focus on high-value, low-effort tasks.
