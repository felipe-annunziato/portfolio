# Business and System Analysis 🧠🔍

This section demonstrates my core skills as a Business and System Analyst, focused on aligning business needs with technology solutions.

---

## 1. Gathering Requirements 📑

- Facilitating workshops and interviews with stakeholders.
- Converting business needs into clear functional and non-functional requirements.
- Documenting user stories and use cases for development teams.

👉 See the `gathering-requirements/` folder for examples.

---

## 2. Mapping & Optimizing Business Processes 🗺️

- Analyzing current (AS-IS) processes and identifying inefficiencies.
- Designing future (TO-BE) workflows to improve performance and value delivery.
- Collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure alignment and adoption.

👉 See the `business-process-mapping/` folder for diagrams and case studies.
